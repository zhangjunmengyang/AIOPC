# Platform icons

These small SVGs are bundled locally so the Accounts page works without third-party image requests. No icon package is installed at runtime.

Most icons come from [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), under [CC0 1.0 Universal](./SIMPLE-ICONS-LICENSE.md). Their original paths are unchanged; a root fill uses the brand color recorded by the upstream project. The pinned upstream file URLs and the individual brand source URLs are recorded in [sources.json](./sources.json).

The LinkedIn icon comes from [Font Awesome Free](https://github.com/FortAwesome/Font-Awesome), copyright Fonticons, Inc., under the icon license [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The bundled [Font Awesome license](./FONT-AWESOME-LICENSE.txt) and the SVG attribution comment are preserved. The path is unchanged and a blue root fill is added. Its pinned source is recorded in sources.json.

The 抖音 icon reuses the TikTok glyph (the same company's mark), and 百家号 uses the Baidu mark. The artwork identifies the corresponding services; trademarks remain with their owners. Platforms without bundled artwork use a text fallback.
